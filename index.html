How it works

  1. Submit a game Use the form above. Your build shows up on this list for the week’s session. After Saturday, the list is wiped. Submitting also means you’ll help playtest others: playtest for playtest.
  2. Join Discord (and optionally sign up) Sessions run in the PakGameDev Discord. Sign up as a playtester if you want reminders; most people both submit and playtest.
  3. Show up Saturday ~6PM (Pakistan Time) Members in Discord stream the live playtests at that time. Browse this list beforehand so you know what’s on, but don’t play the builds early. Keep your first impressions for the stream.
  4. Stream it live and give fresh feedback Play on stream so others hear your unspoiled thoughts in the moment. If you submitted a game, be there while it’s played.
